# The lines below are a working draft; the final scenario will replace them.

== d1
@bg shop
@music shop
: Morning in the potion shop. The bottles on the shelves catch the first light.
heroine: Another quiet morning. Auntie is away until next week, so the shop is all mine.
jaros: All yours, and all mine to watch. Try not to sell the cough syrup as perfume again.
heroine: That happened once!
@show chad
chad: Morning, potion girl. The usual hangover tonic. Make it quick.
heroine: Chad! G-good morning! Here, I made it fresh, just for you.
chad: Hm. You don't have to stare like that. It's weird.
@hide
heroine: He noticed me… sort of.
jaros: He noticed his tonic.
heroine: Jaros, I've decided. I'm going to brew a love potion.
jaros: Absolutely not.
heroine: Auntie's book has the recipe. Six potions mixed together: Fun, Courage, Trust, Hope, Confidence and Gratitude.
jaros: And who will stop you from blowing up the workshop?
heroine: You will. Come on!

== p1 potion fun
@on start
heroine: The Potion of Fun. First of six!
@on tutorial_recipe
jaros: Fine. If you're doing this, do it properly. That page is the recipe: which elements go in, and in what proportion.
@on tutorial_elements
jaros: Everything here is made of four elements: fire, water, earth and air. Each crystal holds some of them.
jaros: The marks on the cauldron count what's inside. Amounts don't matter, only the proportion. One to one means as much fire as earth.
@on tutorial_drag
jaros: Drag a crystal into the cauldron. Go on, it won't bite.
@on tutorial_brew
jaros: When the proportion is right, press Brew. If it's wrong, it fizzles and you start again. No harm done.
@on advice
jaros: Between you and me, the book is too careful. Here's my recipe. A breath of air is what makes it fun.
@on fizzle random
heroine: That's… not a potion.
jaros: Smells like a burnt sock.
heroine: Oops. Again, then.
@on success
heroine: It worked! One down, five to go!
