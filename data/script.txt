# Bonding Potion — game script
# Format: see documents/Technical Design Document.md, section 5.1
#   == d1                    dialogue section
#   == p1 potion fun         potion section (lines grouped by @on <event>)
#   == ending romance        ending section
#   speaker expression: text   (expression is optional)
#   : text                     narration
#   @bg shop  @show chad smile  @hide  @expr heroine sad  @music shop  @sfx meow  @wait 0.5
# Speakers: heroine, chad, jaros, jaros_human (see data/characters.json)
# Potion events: start, tutorial_recipe, tutorial_elements, tutorial_drag, tutorial_brew,
#   advice, hint, resist_heavy, resist_slip, fizzle, success
# The lines below are a working draft; the final scenario will replace them.

== d1
@bg shop
@music shop
: Morning in the potion shop. The bottles on the shelves catch the first light.
heroine happy: Another quiet morning. Auntie is away until next week, so the shop is all mine.
jaros: All yours, and all mine to watch. Try not to sell the cough syrup as perfume again.
heroine embarrassed: That happened once!
@show chad smile
chad smile: Morning, potion girl. The usual hangover tonic. Make it quick.
heroine surprised: Chad! G-good morning! Here, I made it fresh, just for you.
chad neutral: Hm. You don't have to stare like that. It's weird.
@hide
heroine sad: He noticed me… sort of.
jaros: He noticed his tonic.
heroine neutral: Jaros, I've decided. I'm going to brew a love potion.
jaros: Absolutely not.
heroine happy: Auntie's book has the recipe. Six potions mixed together: Fun, Courage, Trust, Hope, Confidence and Gratitude.
jaros: And who will stop you from blowing up the workshop?
heroine happy: You will. Come on!

== p1 potion fun
@on start
heroine happy: The Potion of Fun. First of six!
@on tutorial_recipe
jaros: Fine. If you're doing this, do it properly. That page is the recipe: what goes in, and how much.
@on tutorial_elements
jaros: Everything here is made of four elements: fire, water, earth and air. Each crystal holds some of them.
jaros: The marks on the cauldron count what's inside.
@on tutorial_drag
jaros: Drag a crystal into the cauldron. Go on, it won't bite.
@on tutorial_brew
jaros: When it looks right, press Brew. If it's wrong, it fizzles and you start again. No harm done.
@on advice
jaros: Between you and me, the book is too careful. Three fire, one earth. That's real fun.
@on hint
heroine neutral: Let me look at the recipe again…
@on fizzle random
heroine sad: That's… not a potion.
jaros: Smells like a burnt sock.
heroine embarrassed: Oops. Again, then.
@on success
heroine happy: It worked! One down, five to go!
